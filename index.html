What This Is

Not a hotel.
Not a resort.
A pop-up.

Ash & Moss doesn't have a permanent address. It arrives — fully set up, fully staffed, fully alive — for exactly one month. Then it folds back into the landscape it came from.

A remote village. High altitude. A small house made to feel like yours. WiFi that works, hot water that runs, food cooked fresh every day from what's grown nearby. Everything you need. None of what you don't.

Six guests per run. One group. No strangers dropping in, no one else's schedule to work around. When this month is yours, it is entirely yours. The location is shared once we've spoken — that's part of how this works.
